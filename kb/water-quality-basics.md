## Oxygen solubility and temperature
Warm water holds less oxygen than cold water. In fresh water at sea level, water saturated with oxygen holds about 11.28 mg/L at 10 °C, 9.08 mg/L at 20 °C and 7.54 mg/L at 30 °C. FishFarm uses these values to show oxygen as a percentage of saturation.

## Low-oxygen risk in warm water
Above about 27 °C the risk of low oxygen rises sharply: the water holds less oxygen while fish and bacteria use more of it.
