## Telling a broken probe from a water problem
Real water always moves a little. A probe that reports exactly the same value for an hour or more is almost certainly stuck. A probe whose readings slowly move away from its usual daily pattern is drifting and needs calibration. Sudden jumps that come back on the next reading are spikes, often from a loose cable or air bubbles on the sensor. These are hardware problems for the technician. A value that changes smoothly and agrees with the other probes is a water problem for the worker on shift.

## Probe calibration
pH probes are calibrated with two buffer solutions (pH 7 and pH 10, or pH 4 and 7) about once a month. Oxygen probes need a clean membrane and an air or saturated-water calibration. Temperature probes are checked against a reference thermometer.
