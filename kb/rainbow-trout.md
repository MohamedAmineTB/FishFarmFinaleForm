## Water needs of rainbow trout
Rainbow trout grow best at 13 to 18 °C. They need dissolved oxygen between 80 and 100 % of saturation and do best at pH 7 to 8. FAO describes the best temperature for trout as below 21 °C, with pH 6.5 to 8.5 and oxygen close to saturation.

## Upper temperature limit for trout: sources differ
Sources disagree on the upper temperature trout can survive: some give about 24 °C, others up to 27 °C for short periods. FishFarm keeps the conservative value (24 °C as critical, 20 °C as the warning level).

## Why trout basins dip first at night
Trout need more oxygen than tilapia or carp, so the same night-time oxygen fall that is harmless for warm-water species can push a trout basin under its 7 mg/L warning level.
