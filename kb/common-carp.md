## Water needs of common carp
Common carp grow best at 23 to 30 °C and tolerate pH 6.5 to 9.0. They are among the most tolerant farmed fish for oxygen and can survive levels as low as 0.3 to 0.5 mg/L, but feeding and growth drop when oxygen stays low, so 5 mg/L remains the practical warning level for carp.
