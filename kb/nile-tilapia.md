## Tilapia temperature and oxygen
Nile tilapia is a warm-water fish that prefers 31 to 36 °C and grows poorly below about 22 °C. It is very tolerant of low oxygen and can survive dissolved oxygen below 1 mg/L for short periods, but feeding and growth suffer long before that, so farms keep DO above 5 mg/L.

## Tilapia pH
Tilapia tolerate a wide pH range; farms usually keep pH between 6.5 and 8.5. A high afternoon pH is a sign of strong algae activity.
