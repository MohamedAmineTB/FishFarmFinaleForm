## Toxic ammonia depends on pH and temperature
Fish excrete ammonia. In water it exists as ionized ammonium (NH4+) and un-ionized ammonia (NH3); NH3 is far more toxic. The toxic share rises quickly with pH and temperature: at 24 °C it is a fraction of 0.0502 of total ammonia at pH 8 and 0.3456 at pH 9, about seven times more. That is why a high afternoon pH makes the same amount of ammonia much more dangerous.

## Ammonia and nitrite limits
Un-ionized ammonia above about 0.05 mg/L harms fish, and nitrite becomes toxic from about 0.10 mg/L. Test ammonia about once a week, and more often when pH is high.
