## Feed is the biggest cost
On most fish farms feed is the largest single cost: for US channel catfish it is about 45 to 55 % of production costs. Small improvements in how feed is used therefore move profit more than anything else.

## Feed conversion ratio (FCR)
The FCR is the kilograms of feed needed to produce one kilogram of fish; lower is better. Overfeeding, feeding when oxygen is low and feeding in cold water all raise the FCR, because fish eat less and the rest is wasted.

## Survival and stocking density
More fish per cubic metre means more revenue per basin but also more oxygen demand at night and more waste. Raising stocking density only pays if oxygen stays safe; otherwise mortality and slower growth cancel the gain.
