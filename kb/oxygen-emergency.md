## Emergency actions when oxygen is low
1. Stop feeding for one to three days: digestion raises oxygen demand and uneaten feed rots and uses oxygen.
2. Run the aerators from the evening until after sunrise, when algae start producing oxygen again.
3. In tanks and small ponds, exchange 25 to 50 % of the water with well-oxygenated water if it is available.
4. Check the reading again before dawn: one low reading can be noise, a falling trend is not.
5. If the reading looks impossible (flat for hours, or jumping), suspect the probe and call the technician before acting on it.

## Do not aerate supersaturated afternoon water
In the afternoon, water rich in algae can hold more than 100 % of oxygen saturation. Aerating then pushes oxygen out of the water instead of adding it. Aeration pays off at night and before dawn.
