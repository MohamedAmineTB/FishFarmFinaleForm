## No verified sea bass or sea bream thresholds yet
FishFarm has no verified temperature or pH thresholds for European sea bass and gilthead sea bream. For these species only generic warm-water oxygen levels are used (warning 5 mg/L, critical 3 mg/L), marked as not verified. The assistant says so instead of inventing species values.
